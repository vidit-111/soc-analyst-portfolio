# SOC Analyst Lab Portfolio

Blue-team investigation write-ups documenting hands-on SOC lab work — alert triage, phishing and threat-actor investigation, and digital forensics — completed as part of a deliberate transition into cybersecurity from a product management background.

Each write-up focuses on **methodology and analysis**: how the investigation was approached, what was found, and how findings map to industry frameworks. The aim is to demonstrate the analytical and reporting skills a SOC analyst uses day to day.

> **Disclaimer:** All investigations documented here were carried out in controlled, educational lab environments (TryHackMe SOC Simulator and SOC learning paths). No real systems, organisations, or data are involved. Specific answer values (flags, exact indicator strings, hashes) are redacted or masked in line with the platform's terms of use — the focus is on methodology, not solutions.
